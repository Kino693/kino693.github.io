Plainly Wired is a local publishing workspace for original English-language technology explainers.

The workspace helps its owner research and write episodes, render original diagrams, prepare narration and captions, and upload finished films to the owner's existing YouTube channel. It uses YouTube Analytics and Reporting to evaluate the channel's actual performance.

A tool for one channel owner

This is a personal production tool in development, not a public service for connecting other people's channels. Google authorization is completed by the owner. New uploads start private; publication is enabled only after the project's content and technical checks.

How Google access is used

The app requests YouTube management access to upload and update the owner's videos, thumbnails and captions, and read-only YouTube analytics access. It does not request account passwords or access to Gmail. Credentials and working files stay on the owner's computer.

Narration may use Google's Gemini API. Original scripts and source-compatible audio used for quality review are sent to that service. The site's privacy notice explains this separately from YouTube account access.
